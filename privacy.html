Privacy Policy

Lazy Solitaire · Last updated 2026-08-18

Lazy Solitaire does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Gameplay itself never requires an internet connection.

What the app does

The entire game runs locally on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that knows who you are.

On launch, the app checks a small file on our own server for a newer version of the game's on-screen content (the same update mechanism as visiting our website) and downloads it if one is available, so bug fixes and improvements can reach you without waiting on an app store update. This check contains no information about you or your device, and if it fails — no connection, server unreachable — the app simply continues with whatever version is already installed.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included in the app
  • No cookies or local storage are used
  • No data is shared with any third party, because none is collected in the first place

Our website

The website version of the game (where you're reading this) uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies or any persistent identifier, doesn't track you individually or across sites, and only reports anonymous, aggregated numbers like page views and referring site. This applies to the website only — the native app installed on your device does not include it.

Game state

Your current game exists only in the app's memory while it's open. Nothing about how you play is saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.
