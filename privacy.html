Privacy Policy

Simple Solitaire · Last updated 2026-08-18

Simple Solitaire does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and it does not connect to the internet during play.

What the app does

The entire game runs locally on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server the app talks to.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included
  • No cookies or local storage are used
  • No data is shared with any third party, because none is collected in the first place

Game state

Your current game exists only in the app's memory while it's open. Nothing about how you play is saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.
